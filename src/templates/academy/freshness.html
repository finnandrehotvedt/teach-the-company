Review before publishing.

A daily server process reads allowlisted primary sources through the bounded safe-fetch service, prepares source-evidenced private drafts and claims a small editorial queue. No detected text, generated draft or queue claim can publish itself.

What “fresh” means here

Pages display their genuine reviewed date and version. Source checks show whether upstream text changed. A first baseline is only a comparison point, not content approval. Checks do not prove every claim remains correct. Publication requires a named editorial decision, reviewed lesson change, Git commit, released version and public history record.
